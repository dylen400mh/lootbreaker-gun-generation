# Lootbreaker Gun Generation Steps

---

## Step Zero: Determine Player Tier
Set by the GM based on player level: **Tier 1**, **Tier 2**, or **Tier 3**.
Tier affects damage dice and module chances throughout all steps.

## Step Five: Determine Module Chance

First, roll against the chance table for the weapon's tier and rarity. If the roll succeeds, roll 1d6 on the appropriate guild's module table below.

### Module Chance by Tier & Rarity

| Rarity | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Common | 0% | 0% | 0% |
| Uncommon | 15% | 30% | 60% |
| Rare | 30% | 50% | 70% |
| Epic | 40% | 60% | 80% |
| Legendary | 50% | 75% | 100% |
